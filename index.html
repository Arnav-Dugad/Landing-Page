02About

How I work

I started building because I wanted things that didn't exist yet — a game that played the way I imagined, a tool that removed a chore. That instinct hasn't changed. Every project here began as a small irritation with the way something worked, and ended as software someone can actually open.

I care most about the middle 90% — the part after the idea and before the launch, where the interface either earns trust or quietly loses it. Loading states, empty states, the feel of a button under the cursor. Those aren't polish; they're the product.
